What StarkLLM does

Your private, local-first research companion.

Core Features

  • Local Chat: Talk to open-weight LLMs safely on your own machine without sending data to the cloud.
  • Document Upload & RAG: Upload PDFs and text files directly into your workspace. StarkLLM automatically chunks, embeds, and retrieves relevant context to ground the AI's answers in your actual sources.
  • Knowledge Base: Maintain a global directory of reference material on your computer. Map this directory into StarkLLM to search across all your persistent personal knowledge.
  • Multi-Workspace Support: Isolate contexts by project. Keep your coding tasks separated from your literature reviews.
  • Source-Grounded Answers: Never wonder where an answer came from. StarkLLM provides direct context from the documents you provide it.

Under the Hood

StarkLLM is built for technical users and researchers. It runs locally using Docker Desktop to orchestrate the backend and frontend, and connects seamlessly to Ollama running on your host machine for fast, secure LLM inference.

This architecture guarantees that the product remains transparent, inspectable, and completely private.
