How StarkLLM Works

An end-to-end local pipeline designed with data privacy in mind.

The Document Lifecycle

When you upload a PDF or text file into StarkLLM, it is processed locally. Local parsers extract the raw text (using OCR if necessary). Second, a Semantic Chunking algorithm splits the text into logical, overlapping blocks. Third, a local embedding model converts these blocks into high-dimensional vectors, storing them in your private ChromaDB container. Finally, when you ask a question, the system retrieves the most relevant vectors and injects them into the context window of your chosen Ollama LLM, aiming to produce grounded responses.

Verifiable Intelligence

Large Language Models are prone to hallucination when relying solely on their pre-trained weights. StarkLLM solves this through rigorous Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

  • Source-Backed Responses: The AI is prompted to read your exact documents before formulating an answer, aiming for high fidelity to the original text.
  • Direct Citations: Every response includes expandable citations. Click a citation to view the exact paragraph the AI used to construct its logic.
  • Reduced Hallucination: By constraining the model's context to your verified data, you significantly reduce the risk of confident but false information.

Your Persistent Library

While workspace uploads are great for isolated projects, the Global Knowledge Base acts as your permanent, universally accessible reference library.

  • Local Folder Indexing: Map any folder on your Windows machine to StarkLLM. The system will recursively index supported files into a global vector collection.
  • Universal Access: Query your Knowledge Base from any active chat session, regardless of the current workspace you are in.
  • Continuous Synchronization: As you add or modify files in your local folder, the Knowledge Base updates to reflect your latest research.

Workspace vs Knowledge Base

StarkLLM offers two ways to contextualize the AI. Understanding the difference is critical to organizing your intelligence.

Workspace Documents

  • Tied strictly to one single workspace.
  • Useful when work must stay isolated to prevent context contamination.
  • Best for temporary or highly specific document sets.
Example:
A folder of contracts for one specific legal case.

Knowledge Base

  • A persistent, global intelligence layer.
  • Useful across all chats and ongoing workspaces simultaneously.
  • Best for long-term reference material and personal archives.
Example:
Your ongoing, permanent research library or company SOPs.

Local Control

StarkLLM provides deep administrative control over your local inference environment, empowering you to tune performance to your exact hardware capabilities.

  • Model Selection: Switch seamlessly between any LLM downloaded via Ollama (e.g., Llama 3, Mistral, Qwen) directly from the chat interface.
  • Runtime Configuration: Adjust context window sizes, temperature, and advanced decoding parameters to perfectly balance speed and intelligence.
  • Local-First Routing: Review your network settings to verify that API calls are routed to localhost, helping keep your workflows private.
