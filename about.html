About StarkLLM

The mission for private, inspectable AI.

What StarkLLM Is

StarkLLM is a private, local-first AI assistant for Windows. It lets you chat with open-weight Large Language Models (LLMs) and interrogate your own documents using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Every process runs locally on your machine—intended to keep chats and documents on the local machine during normal local operation.

This is a free Research Beta. It is highly capable for technical users, but it is not a polished, commercial SaaS product. You are responsible for providing capable hardware, choosing your models via Ollama, and evaluating the outcomes of your AI interactions.

Why It Exists

The standard paradigm of AI requires handing your sensitive documents—legal cases, medical records, or proprietary code—to remote corporate servers. StarkLLM was built to prove that you can achieve powerful, document-grounded AI intelligence while maintaining strong data privacy.

Who It Is For

StarkLLM is built for researchers, developers, legal professionals, and privacy advocates who require a secure environment for document analysis and refuse to compromise on data privacy.
