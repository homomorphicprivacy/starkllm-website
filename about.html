About StarkLLM

The mission for private, inspectable AI.

Our Mission

StarkLLM was created with a singular focus: bringing powerful AI capabilities entirely to your local machine. We believe that your private documents, research notes, and daily thoughts shouldn't have to be sent to a corporate cloud just to interact with an AI.

By providing a completely free and open source tool, we aim to foster transparency and empower technical researchers and developers to own their AI workflows.
